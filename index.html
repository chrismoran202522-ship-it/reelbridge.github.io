❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the automation work? +
Our platform uses advanced AI and machine learning to handle content creation, scheduling, engagement, and analytics completely autonomously. Once you set your preferences and connect your accounts, the system runs 24/7 without requiring manual intervention.
Can I change my contract length later? +
Yes! You can upgrade your contract length at any time to unlock better rates. When you extend, the new discounted rate applies to your next billing cycle. You can also downgrade with 30 days notice.
Are there any setup fees? +
No setup fees for Starter, Growth, and Professional packages. Enterprise includes a dedicated onboarding specialist. Optional add-on onboarding is available for $599 if you need extra training.
What if I exceed my post limit? +
The system will automatically notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can either upgrade your package or purchase additional post packs as add-ons. Unused posts don't roll over to the next month.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial on all packages with full access to features. No credit card required to start. You can upgrade to a paid contract anytime during or after the trial.
How do I cancel? +
Monthly plans can be canceled anytime with 7 days notice. Longer contracts can be downgraded to monthly with 30 days notice, or you can pause your account for up to 3 months without losing data.
