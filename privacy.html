Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Reel Bridge ("we", "us", "our") operates the website reelbridge.site (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information (email, name, password hash)
  • Social media account credentials (OAuth tokens – stored securely, never shared)
  • Payment information (handled by Stripe/PayPal – we do not store card details)
  • Content you schedule or generate through the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Service
  • Authenticate and connect your social media accounts
  • Process payments and subscriptions
  • Send service-related communications
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Social media platforms (only with your explicit OAuth consent)
  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures (HTTPS, token encryption, secure hosting). However, no method is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting support@reelbridge.site.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.

8. Contact Us
